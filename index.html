How to Play

1

Objective

Transform all words in the grid to the target word by applying linguistic transformations in the correct sequence.

2

Transformations

Each level has a fixed sequence of transformations. You must apply them in order. Each transformation only affects words that meet specific structural or phonetic constraints.

3

Constraints

A transformation will only apply to words that meet its constraint (e.g., "must end with H", "must contain vowel U"). Pay attention to which words change!

4

Power-Ups

🔮 Sound Peek - Reveals upcoming constraints
⏪ Rewind - Undo your last move
🧮 Morpheme Map - Shows word structure
